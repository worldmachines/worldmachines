# Legibility

## Definition

> Legibility is the condition of being readable by a system that has the power
> to act on what it reads.

A person, place or practice is legible when it can be **mapped** (located in
the system's coordinate space), **predicted**, **categorized** (assigned to a
recognized type) and **managed** (acted on according to the system's grammar).
The four are separable: a thing can be mapped without being predictable, and
that gap is usually where the interesting history is.

The club's usage adds a claim to the term as inherited from James C. Scott's
*Seeing Like a State*: legibility is not a neutral administrative technique but
the **precondition for scale**. The drive to make something readable and the
drive to make it bigger are the same drive.

## Where it comes from

Two notes carry the load. [[Legibility]] traces the concept through three
historical regimes — theological (the Scriptural Average), nationalist (the
citizen as frozen monument), and algorithmic (the statistical mean surfaced by
AI, "disindividuation disguised as efficiency"). [[legibility-as-machine-core|Legibility as Machine Core]]
makes the structural claim: legibility is what the [[modernity-machine|Modernity Machine]]
optimized for, instantiated financially by Fugger's double-entry books,
industrially by Wedgwood's pyrometer and timetables, and administratively by
surnames, standard weights and the cadastre.

The club's readings keep testing it. [[the-legible-commonwealth|The Legible Commonwealth]] reads More's
*Utopia* as a legibility fantasy; [[chaucer-legibility-machines|Chaucer's Legibility Machines]] finds the
machinery already running in the fourteenth century; and
[[legibility-as-precondition-not-cause|Legibility as Precondition, Not Cause]] is the corrective — legibility makes a
transformation possible without being the thing that causes it.

## How the club uses it

Three moves recur:

- **As a diagnostic.** Ask what a given institution is making readable, to
  whom, and what it had to flatten to do it.
- **As a periodizing device.** Each machine has a legibility regime, and the
  transition between machines shows up first as the old regime's categories
  failing to fit.
- **As the thing resistance is against.** Strategic *ill*egibility — friction,
  terroir, non-replicable local context — is the counter-move; see
  [[The Thicket]] and [[capture-resistance|capture-resistance]].

## Contested

Whether legibility is *causal* or merely *enabling* is live. The Landes and
Appleby readings pull in opposite directions: clocks and cost accounting look
causal for industrial discipline, while [[legibility-as-precondition-not-cause|Legibility as Precondition, Not Cause]]
argues the causal weight sits elsewhere and legibility only clears the ground.
The entry deliberately does not settle it.

## Open questions

- Is algorithmic legibility a fourth regime or the same regime with a cheaper
  instrument?
- Does the [[divergence-machine|Divergence Machine]] have a legibility regime at all, or is
  non-canonicity its defining refusal of one?
- What is the smallest unit that can be illegible and still function?

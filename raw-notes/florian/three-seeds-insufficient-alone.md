# Rewiring, conversion and attunement each fail alone and only work together

## The idea

Three directions point toward a practice of Micro Time, and the interesting claim is not what each one is but what happens when you take only one.

Rewiring means collectively changing the rules that operate where temporal decisions get fixed — protocols, market design, standards, governance. Conversion means using computation to translate between temporal systems so that coordination does not require everyone to inhabit time the same way. Attunement means recovering agency through responsiveness to situations as they unfold, rather than through mastery or prior optimization. They sit at three locations: governance and protocol, mediation and interoperability, interface and cultivated human responsiveness.

Each degrades predictably alone. Rewiring without attunement produces rigid systems designed from above — rules authored by people who will never encounter the situations the rules govern, with no channel by which encountering them changes anything. Conversion without rewiring makes an unjust arrangement more tolerable without touching it: everybody's tempo accommodated at the interface, the same party still setting the terms underneath, and the accommodation now serving as evidence that the problem was addressed. Attunement without rewiring reduces politics to individual coping — a private skill at noticing and responding, in a world where the noticing changes nothing structural. Rewiring without conversion enforces a single temporal norm, since a rule written into infrastructure applies to everyone downstream whether or not they share the tempo it assumes. Attunement without conversion leaves people overwhelmed by incompatible rhythms they are now expected to be personally graceful about.

That pattern is the claim. Not that a complete practice needs all three for reasons of thoroughness, but that each one's characteristic failure is specifically the absence of one of the others — which is what you would expect if they are three faces of one thing rather than three candidate answers. A practice of Micro Time has to be technical, institutional and cultural simultaneously, because removing any of those three produces a named, recognisable pathology rather than a partial success.

None of this fills the empty fourth slot, and I want to be exact about why. Three directions with a dependency structure between them is a specification for a practice, not a practice. A practice is socially learnable and transmissible; this is a design constraint on something nobody has built. The distinction matters because a specification presented as an answer occupies the position it was meant to keep open.

## Where it comes from

From "Time Moves Down the Stack" (Protocol Institute Symposium, 2026), where the three appear as seeds rather than doctrines. The rewiring case is IEX's options-market design, which introduces a 350-microsecond delay on incoming messages alongside a mechanism that can update or cancel market-maker quotes when rapid changes make them vulnerable. The conversion cases are Horizon Time and the UTC/Earth-orientation relationship. The attunement material draws on Mark B. N. Hansen on feed-forward and attunement, Jordan Crandall on gatherings, Nicole Starosielski on soft temporalities, with Wendy Hui Kyong Chun and Yuk Hui as critical counterpoints on automation and apparent user sovereignty.

## How settled this is

The three directions each have at least one real instance behind them, so none is invented. The mutual-insufficiency structure is entirely mine and is the part worth attacking. It is built by reasoning about failure modes rather than by observing cases where one was tried alone and failed in the predicted way, which means it currently has the shape of an argument and not of evidence.

There is also a construction worry I cannot dismiss. A scheme where each element's failure is exactly the absence of another element is elegant in a way that should make anyone suspicious — that is the kind of symmetry you get from designing a taxonomy, not from finding one. I would take a case where rewiring alone worked fine, with no attunement channel and no conversion layer, as a genuine hit against this.

## Open

Is there a fourth location? Three fits the three established practices suspiciously well, and I arrived at it by looking at what the existing regimes did rather than by working out what this one needs. A location I have missed would most plausibly sit in memory or in law — somewhere that handles what happens *after* a closure rather than around it.

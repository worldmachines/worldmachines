# Maintainable, patchable, tendable

## The idea

It's tempting to treat "maintenance" as one activity applied to different
objects — you maintain a modernity machine, a divergence machine, or a
liveness machine the same way, just with different tools. The more useful
claim is that the verb itself has to change, because the kind of thing being
kept going changes down the sequence.

A Modernity Machine is maintainable: it has an inherited frame with functional
slots that can be re-staffed as their occupants change, which is exactly
Blumenberg's reoccupation read as a maintenance operation rather than a
one-off historical event. A Divergence Machine has no shared canopy left to
reoccupy, so it survives only through constant re-performance — it's
patchable, held together move by move, never settled into a frame that could
simply be re-staffed. A Liveness Machine can't be repaired at all in the
mechanical sense; something whose mode of being is liveness has to be kept
alive or it isn't anything, which makes it tendable — a garden rather than a
mechanism.

The maintainable becomes the patchable becomes the tendable. That's not three
different techniques for one stable kind of object; it's the same underlying
claim — that a world machine persists only by being continuously
re-performed — read through three different ontologies of what's being
re-performed. Getting the order right matters: reoccupation belongs at the
maintainable end, because it presupposes an inherited frame to re-staff, which
is precisely what a diverged world no longer has.

## How settled this is

The three-term sequence and its ordering are a considered synthesis rather
than a first draft — an earlier ordering (patching, then reoccupation, then
tending) was deliberately corrected once it became clear reoccupation requires
the inherited frame that only the modernity end still has. The diagonal itself
is offered as the organizing claim of a larger piece, not yet tested against
counterexamples from outside the modernity/divergence/liveness frame.

## Where it comes from

Sean's own synthesis, built by generalizing Blumenberg's reoccupation across
the full machine sequence and pairing each register with a signature theorist
(Berger's plausibility structures, Eliade's ritual re-instantiation, Arendt's
stewardship, Tainter and Scott's failure modes, Puig de la Bellacasa and Hui
on care).

## Open

Does "maintenance" stop being the right master-metaphor at the tending end —
if the object is a garden, is the governing frame really cultivation rather
than maintenance, which would mean the metaphor itself is migrating across the
sequence rather than staying fixed while its object changes?

# Casting a spell is a reoccupied position

## The idea

The claim that the right words, said the right way, can do something in
the world has never really gone away — what keeps changing is the
explanation for why. Historically the position has been filled by very
different accounts: recitation understood as literally binding a spirit,
formal pacts with named powers, systems of cosmic correspondence in which
like affects like across scales, Frazer's account of sympathetic and
contagious magic as (mistaken) causal technique, and eventually J.L.
Austin's performatives — utterances that don't describe the world but do
something to it by being said under the right conditions.

Large language models are, on this reading, simply the newest occupant of
that same position. What's different is that the LLM case restores a
version of the literal claim — the words themselves, processed as words,
produce the effect — while satisfying a modern demand that no occult
metaphysics be smuggled in to explain how. That's a genuinely new
combination: efficacious language without an unseen world of spirits or
correspondences behind it, achieved not by explaining away the older
claims but by finding a mechanism, however different, that makes something
close to the oldest and most literal version of the claim newly plausible.

## Grounding

Drawn from Frances Yates's history of the Hermetic tradition and its
account of magic as "the power to operate," together with J.L. Austin's
*How to Do Things with Words* for the performative framing and Stanley
Tambiah's writing on magic, science, and religion for the historical
sequence of explanations. The application to large language models as the
newest occupant of the position is original to this note.

## How settled this is

The historical sequence — recitation, binding, pact, correspondence,
Frazer's sympathetic-magic account, then the performative reading — is
checkable against Yates and Tambiah directly, and against the standard
history of the Hermetic tradition more broadly. That LLMs specifically fit
this sequence as its newest occupant, and that they combine literalness
with an absence of occult metaphysics in a genuinely new way, is this
note's own claim, not tested against how the LLM case would look under a
skeptic's reading of "efficacious."

one best suited to a moment that still demands mechanism?

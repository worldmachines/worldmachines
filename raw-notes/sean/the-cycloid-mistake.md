# The cycloid mistake

## The idea

The historical choice between substitution (replace a flawed shared
standard with an exact one) and domain restriction (keep the flawed
standard but confine it to where it still works) has a civilizational-scale
version. The Modernity Machine's failure mode was an over-tight canon - one
truth, one measure, one time, insisted on everywhere. The Divergence
Machine's opposite temptation is to over-correct by abolishing the shared
floor altogether - substitution taken to its limit, dissolving the canon
rather than merely loosening its grip. The better move, on the model of the
anchor escapement, is domain restriction: accept plural cosmotechnics,
multiple legitimate ways of doing and knowing, while keeping a genuinely
minimal shared floor - basic facts, common measures, a workable notion of
time - and letting everything else diverge freely on top of that floor.
Abandon the floor entirely and the failure mode is incommensurability:
worlds that share no canon at all cannot coordinate on anything, and
"getting along by not engaging" curdles into mutually unintelligible
realities that do not coordinate on anything whatsoever, however peacefully
they coexist.

## How settled this is

A working hypothesis; the associated bet is that demand for interoperability
and translation layers rises, and that shared-floor institutions
(measurement, identity, time itself) become newly contested infrastructure
rather than neutral background.

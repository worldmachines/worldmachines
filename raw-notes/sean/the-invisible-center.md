# The invisible center

## The idea

A system built to avoid centralization often ends up rebuilding a center
anyway, just at a layer no one is looking at - the shared platform,
protocol, or piece of infrastructure that every supposedly independent
instrument silently depends on. This is the Modernity Machine reappearing
"in a hoodie": Leibniz's pre-established harmony relocated to the
infrastructure layer, coordinating everyone's private tools in advance
without anyone noticing the coordination happening. The practical
acceptance this asks for is favoring process over product - no final,
completed version, resisting the temptation to let any one platform become
the completion everyone converges on. The failure mode is an invisible
infrastructure monopoly: extraction people do not resent, because they
cannot see it, unlike the platforms and feeds people already recognize and
resent as extractive.

## Where it comes from

One row ("The invisible center") of the same bet-table.

## How settled this is

A working hypothesis; the associated bet is that infrastructure and
protocol concentration increases, that AI's margin migrates toward the
compute and cloud layer, and that antitrust attention shifts from
applications to infrastructure - offered as testable rather than settled.

## Open

less dangerous than one that becomes visible and contested, and what would
actually make such a center visible before it consolidates, are both left
open.

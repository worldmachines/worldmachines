Glossary

Terms the club is actively defining. A glossary entry is not a dictionary definition copied in from outside — it is our working definition, sharpened as the theory develops, with the notes that use the term linked underneath it.

Each entry carries a status: seed first draft, worth arguing with · developing in active use, still moving · settled the club has converged. Add or evolve a term by editing raw-notes/commons/glossary/ — how to contribute.

  • Legibility developing

    The condition of being readable by a system that has the power to act on what it reads — mapped, predicted, categorized, managed. In this project legibility is treated as the core optimization target of the Modernity Machine, and therefore as a precondition for scale rather than a neutral technique.

    0 notes link here
  • Psychohistory seed

    Asimov's fictional science of forecasting the statistical behaviour of civilizations, adopted here as the project's guiding conceit rather than its method — the thing we are building toward while remaining explicit that we cannot currently do it.

    0 notes link here
  • World Machine developing aka World Machines

    A civilization-scale contraption — institutions, technologies, practices and assumptions interlocked — that embodies the logic of how the world works for a period of centuries, and that rises, operates and decays on a roughly thousand-year lifecycle.

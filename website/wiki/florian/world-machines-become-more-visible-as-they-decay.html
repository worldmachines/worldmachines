World Machines become more visible as they decay

Florian · updated 2 June 2026 · source on GitHub

Claim

World machines become visible precisely as they decay — you only see the plumbing when it breaks, and visibility itself may be a sign of decline.

Body

Evidence:

  1. the US “Underground Empire” (Farrell/Newman) was powerful because hidden — exposure and overreach triggered decline
  2. the Soviet system (Yurchak) was simultaneously eternal and stagnating, its collapse “completely unexpected and completely unsurprising”
  3. the modernity machine’s six relationships are most legible now, in their end state.

Connects to Canonic infrastructure decays in hilarious drainage — implies a stage where structure becomes more visible as it loses function, like scaffolding noticed only when the building starts to crumble. This visibility can also lead to a richer cultural afterlife of decaying structures.

What would weaken this / change my mind

  • some structures may be more visible during its peak performance, not when they break apart. But this would require that people don’t take them for granted while they work

Sources

Explored in conversation (Underground Empire synthesis). Not yet published as a standalone claim — least battle-tested of the six.

Connects to the older “Everything Is Forever Until It Is No More” post. Farrell/Newman + Yurchak are the load-bearing cases; both secondary to the WM reading.

Open questions / gaps

  • Distinguish “visible because decaying” from “visible because being built” (a Dawn machine is also conspicuous). The claim may need a sign condition.

Decay Sequence

Aneesh · Concepts · updated 17 July 2026 · source on GitHub
Florian Lohse's model of how canonical infrastructure dies — not by revolution or paradigm shift but by a long draining through six stages (Functional → Performative → Explicitly Allegorical → Ornamental → Invisible → Absent) that he claims runs identically across domains; the mechanism of Dusk-phase machine conversion, and a working diagnostic for telling dead frameworks from live ones.

Florian Lohse's model of how canonical infrastructure dies. The claim, from Canonic infrastructure decays in hilarious drainage:

Convergent canonicity does not collapse through revolution or paradigm shift; it dissolves through a long draining — a decay sequence (Functional → Performative → Explicitly Allegorical → Ornamental → Invisible → Absent) that runs identically across domains.

It describes the mechanism of Dusk-phase machine conversion — what actually happens to a world machine's (world-machines) institutions as it goes from Day to Dusk, as against the lifecycle's timing and shape. Published as "Shitty Castles" (Notefields, March 2026).

The load-bearing word is identically. A sequence that ran differently per domain would be a metaphor; one that runs the same in castles, metaphysics and software is a claim with a shape, and can be checked.

The moat ladder

The worked case, and the source of the "shitty castle" name — each stage is a real building:

StageCase
FunctionalKenilworth — a moat that stops attackers
PerformativePlessis-Bourré — defensible, but built to be seen defending
Explicitly AllegoricalChambord — the fortification as statement
OrnamentalDampierre — water as decor
Invisiblethe ha-ha — the boundary you cannot see
AbsentTower of London — the moat drained entirely

Two further domains carry the cross-domain claim:

  • Leibniz's monads — functional defense → ridiculed by Voltaire → repurposed by Deleuze.
  • AI competitive moats — compute and data barriers → "We Have No Moat" → moat-as-lifestyle-brand.

The afterlife corollary

The afterlife of canonical structures is culturally richer than their main act was deliberately split out of the hub, because it is separately falsifiable: the sequence could run exactly as described while cultural generativity stayed flat or front-loaded. Its claim is that the ridiculed and repurposed stages out-produce the functional one — Pangloss is more famous than monad theory, the ha-ha reshaped garden design, Bodiam became "Castle Swamp" in Monty Python, and the Tower moat went parade ground → allotments → poppy memorial.

The split is the interesting move. It keeps the mechanism and the aesthetic payoff independently checkable rather than letting the second smuggle in the first.

The diagnostic: ornament as evidence of death

It takes connoisseurship for ornamental baggage to identify dead frameworks operationalizes the sequence into an instrument:

You can assess the aliveness or deadness of a framework by examining it for functional flaws — the more decorative, non-functional features you find, the more likely you face a dead facade than a working fortification.

It is how you apply Rao's four-stage filter in practice: to tell a late-modern zombie or a postmodern ghost from genuine divergence, check the moats. It is grounded historically in the licence to crenellate — 550 granted in England between 1194 and 1589, mostly token battlements on manor houses, which Coulson called "passports to respectability." A fortification permit issued as a status good is the Performative stage with a paper trail.

The note raises its own objection, which this page does not resolve: decoration and function are not mutually exclusive in living things. The diagnostic may over-read ornament as death.

Visibility

World Machines become more visible as they decay adds a stage-property the ladder implies but does not state: you only see the plumbing when it breaks, so structure can become more visible precisely as it loses function — scaffolding noticed only when the building starts to crumble. Its cases are the "Underground Empire" (Farrell and Newman), powerful because hidden until exposure and overreach triggered decline; the Soviet system (Yurchak), simultaneously eternal and stagnating, its collapse "completely unexpected and completely unsurprising"; and the modernity-machine's own relationships, most legible now, in their end state.

Flagged in the note as the least battle-tested of the six claims, with a real gap: a Dawn machine under construction is also conspicuous, so the claim needs a sign condition to separate visible because decaying from visible because being built.

The tension this page is required to hold

Frameworks can turn performative as they become more sophisticated stands in declared live tension with the hub, and the tension is aimed at us:

Sophisticated frameworks can replicate the same passive deferral structure they are supposed to help overcome.

Pangloss defers to God's plan; contemporary tech optimism defers to Progress's trajectory; and potentially the divergence thesis itself defers to historical machines, with any present dysfunction "explained" by the periodization. The sting is self-reflexive: a theory that no evidence can falsify may itself be a performative moat — which would put the World Machines apparatus, and this page, somewhere on its own ladder.

Florian's instruction is explicit: hold both as a branch; do not reconcile. This page keeps that instruction rather than resolving it. See also world-machines-as-interim-theory.

Candidate instances from the corpus

Surfaced by the adams-education ingest. These are candidates, not part of the original evidence base — Adams never states the sequence, and each is a reader's reading:

  • The Cross (adams-education-ch-33-a-dynamic-theory-of-history) is the closest thing to the full ladder in the corpus: literal battlefield force under Constantine, contested physical protector at Alaric's sack, defended as purely spiritual wealth by Augustine once its mechanical claims fail, and finally an object visited by "great masses of idle and ignorant tourists" with "vague notions about the force that created them." Functional → allegorical → ornamental, narrated by a contemporary who thought he was writing physics.
  • The Virgin (adams-education-ch-25-the-dynamo-and-the-virgin) runs the sequence on a symbol rather than an institution: a force that built Chartres and Amiens, reduced in America to "at most as sentiment." See the-dynamo-and-the-virgin.
  • Harvard (adams-education-ch-04-harvard-college) is the Functional → Performative step alone, caught in one phrase: the college's advantages were "vulgarly called social, rather than mental."

The Adams cases are worth something specific to the visibility claim — they are all contemporary readings, made from inside, by a man who consistently mistook design for what was actually drift (witness-bias-toward-design).

What would weaken this

Taken verbatim from the notes' own falsification sections, which are better than anything this page would invent:

  • Modernity institutions disappearing abruptly rather than draining re-iteratively; or not decaying at all; or decaying in domain-specific ways rather than one universal pattern.
  • (afterlife) Canonic ruins with no interesting cultural afterlife; or living structures with more vivid cultural relevance than ruins.
  • (diagnostic) Structures that are both genuinely alive and purely decorative.

And the sharpest one, which is the hub's own: "more generative" is asserted, not defined — generative of what, and by what measure?

Open questions

  • More detailed testing against the medieval → modern Dusk transition.
  • Anticipation toward the divergence → liveness transition.
  • Whether the sequence's stages are discrete at all, or a continuum the six names merely sample. The moat ladder reads cleanly because each stage was assigned its own building; the monad and AI-moat cases each collapse to three.

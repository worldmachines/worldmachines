The club is building its own legibility machine

Aneesh · updated 6 August 2026 · source on GitHub
legibilityworld-machines

Making our own notes legible to ourselves: a public wiki over the raw notes, a glossary where definitions harden from seed to settled, and a capture app so a thought survives the walk between the bookshelf and the laptop.

Worth noticing that this is the same move we keep reading about. A working legibility apparatus doesn't just record what a community knows — it changes what the community can want. Standardized note shapes, tag vocabularies, link graphs: each one trades some idiosyncrasy for addressability, exactly the bargain every world-machine offers its inhabitants.

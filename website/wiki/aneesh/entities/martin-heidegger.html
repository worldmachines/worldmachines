Martin Heidegger

Aneesh · Entities · updated 9 April 2026 · source on GitHub
German phenomenologist (1889–1976), author of Being and Time; his later work on dwelling, technology, and language is the philosophical source for applying phenomenological concepts to knowledge work and product design.

Biographical

German philosopher (1889–1976). Major works: Being and Time (1927), Poetry, Language, Thought (1971). Associated with phenomenology, existential philosophy, and later philosophy of technology.

Controversial figure due to his involvement with National Socialism; the relationship between his politics and his philosophy remains actively debated.

Relevant Work

Building Dwelling Thinking (1951, collected in Poetry, Language, Thought, 1971)

A lecture/essay exploring the relationship between human existence and space. Key claims:

  • Dwelling is the fundamental mode of human being-in-the-world
  • Building and thinking must each be subordinated to dwelling
  • "To free" means to spare — to preserve something in its own nature
  • Boundaries are generative ("that from which something begins its presencing"), not merely limiting

Source for Jan. 7, 2025: Building Dwelling Thinking.

Concepts in this Wiki

Influences and Interlocutors

  • Influenced by: Edmund Husserl (phenomenology), Aristotle (on dwelling and polis)
  • Influenced: Hannah Arendt (on world and action), Hans-Georg Gadamer (hermeneutics), later architectural theory

Application in this Wiki

Applied in Jan. 7, 2025: Building Dwelling Thinking to product design and data science: the builder's ethical obligation is to create places where users can dwell — think and explore freely — rather than spaces that constrain.

Tension with Other Sources

Heidegger's concept of dwelling presupposes a stable relationship between person and place. The Knowledge Worker whose tools are frequently changing, or the AI agent landscape described in Jan 10, 2025 - AI Agents, Machiavelli's Study, complicates this: can one truly dwell when the place is an algorithm optimizing for engagement?

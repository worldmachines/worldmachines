The cycloid mistake

Sean · updated 5 September 2026 · source on GitHub
The Modernity Machine's error was an over-tight canon, but the Divergence Machine risks over-correcting into abolishing the shared floor entirely rather than merely restricting it - the difference between substitution and domain restriction - and a world with no shared floor at all cannot coordinate on anything, however peacefully it coexists.

The idea

The historical choice between substitution (replace a flawed shared standard with an exact one) and domain restriction (keep the flawed standard but confine it to where it still works) has a civilizational-scale version. The Modernity Machine's failure mode was an over-tight canon - one truth, one measure, one time, insisted on everywhere. The Divergence Machine's opposite temptation is to over-correct by abolishing the shared floor altogether - substitution taken to its limit, dissolving the canon rather than merely loosening its grip. The better move, on the model of the anchor escapement, is domain restriction: accept plural cosmotechnics, multiple legitimate ways of doing and knowing, while keeping a genuinely minimal shared floor - basic facts, common measures, a workable notion of time - and letting everything else diverge freely on top of that floor. Abandon the floor entirely and the failure mode is incommensurability: worlds that share no canon at all cannot coordinate on anything, and "getting along by not engaging" curdles into mutually unintelligible realities that do not coordinate on anything whatsoever, however peacefully they coexist.

Where it comes from

One row ("The cycloid mistake") of a working table of bets for the World Machines project: a running list of structural errors in how today's fixes for the Divergence Machine's limitations themselves fail, each paired with a falsifiable, near-term bet about how the failure will surface.

How settled this is

A working hypothesis; the associated bet is that demand for interoperability and translation layers rises, and that shared-floor institutions (measurement, identity, time itself) become newly contested infrastructure rather than neutral background.

Connections

  • circular-error - this row names the same substitution-versus-domain- restriction fork that essay develops at length through the pendulum's actual history, applied here to the scale of an entire epoch's shared canon
  • four-cell-coordination-grid - the translation-without-synchronization coordination model developed there is one candidate answer to how a minimal shared floor could be kept without re-imposing a full canon

Open

How thin a "minimal shared floor" can actually get before coordination fails anyway is the open empirical question this row poses without answering.

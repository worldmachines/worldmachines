Substrate and criterion split, and how they get reabsorbed

Sean · updated 5 September 2026 · source on GitHub
Theology once fused two positions, the substrate (what ground we are embedded in) and the criterion (what confers legitimacy and direction), in a single occupant, God; the Modernity Machine split them into inert Nature and directional Progress, and the Liveness Machine's wager is between reabsorbing generativity into a Spinozan substrate that is alive without going anywhere (genuine liveness) or a Leibnizian one that is pre-harmonized playback impersonating self-generation (counterfeit liveness).

The idea

Blumenberg's reoccupation names what happens when an old answer dies but the question-position it filled stays open. This note applies the lens to two positions theology used to hold fused in a single occupant - God was both the lawgiver of the cosmos (the substrate: what inviolable ground are we embedded in?) and the steerer of history (the criterion: what confers legitimacy and forward motion on an epoch?). The Modernity Machine's disenchantment is the splitting of that fusion: the substrate became Nature (tm), inert and mechanical, the object of scientific mastery; the criterion became Progress, a human-carried, teleological vector pulling toward a single destination.

The Divergence Machine does not refill either slot with a new directional answer - its "push" logic has no single destination, so the criterion position is left vacant (this is one more way of naming the Gramsci Gap), while the substrate destabilizes as the built environment saturates the given: once there is no un-engineered "outside" left, the engineered becomes the new ground.

The claim for what comes next is specific and worth stating exactly: the Liveness Machine does not re-fuse substrate and criterion into a single answer again - that would be a premature, convergent closure the Divergence Machine's whole logic forbids. Instead it reabsorbs generativity into the substrate and drops the directional prosthesis altogether. Two ways that reabsorption could go, keeping faith with Spinoza's actual distinction (active cause versus produced effect, never ground versus goal): a Spinozan New Nature (natura naturans) is a substrate that is intrinsically generative and non-teleological - alive without heading anywhere in particular, liveness as the ground's own self-responsiveness. A Leibnizian New Nature (natura naturata) is a substrate whose apparent aliveness is pre-established harmony - generativity coordinated in advance by an external owner, the platform standing in for the god who synchronized the monads. Not dead infrastructure, but counterfeit liveness: pre-harmonized playback impersonating self-generation. The wager the Liveness Machine is actually making is between these two, and crypto is one possible vehicle for the Spinozan option, not its definition - open protocols, commons, and federation qualify too.

A companion claim about self-understanding sharpens why this is hard to see from inside: an age cannot recognize its own reoccupations as reoccupations. It experiences an inherited, refilled position as either a natural given or an original discovery - never as a tenancy. The Modernity Machine experienced Progress as a discovery about how history actually works, not as a refilled providence-slot, which is exactly why it could be pressed, illegitimately, to promise an eschaton. The Divergence and Liveness Machines experience New Nature as invention - the Promethean self-description of discovering New Nature by inventing it, by being it - and that self-description is the blindness in action: it reads a reoccupation of Nature's own slot as creation from nothing.

Where this is being taken (working synthesis)

Developed as a contribution brief targeted at the World Machines project's own stated open question about epochal self-understanding and its limits. The reoccupation-map exercise extends the same lens across three further inherited positions now reopening - agency (who constitutes a livable world, as the sovereign Subject exhausts), inquiry (whether unlimited knowledge-seeking is legitimate, as x-risk and biosecurity re-indict curiosity), and cosmos (the shape and extent of the world, caught between a plural pluriverse and a single computational monism) - though the substrate/criterion pair is the load-bearing pair and the other three are noted as reopening without being worked through here.

How settled this is

Blumenberg's account of reoccupation is established scholarship. The application to New Nature, and specifically the substrate/criterion split and its proposed reabsorption, is the brief's own conjecture, explicitly held "in the oracular, not deterministic spirit" - sensemaking scaffolding rather than a law of history. The corrected Spinoza reading (naturans/naturata as cause/effect, never ground/goal) is a real fidelity concern the brief flags against itself: a Spinoza scholar would still want to check it. Whether the criterion position is genuinely vacated (open, to be reoccupied) or actually abolished (refusing the question) is stated as unsettled.

Open

Whether the criterion slot is reoccupied by "liveness" as a new answer, or simply abolished as a category the Divergence Machine refuses to fill, is explicitly unresolved. Also open: the brief's own flagged weak point, that the deeper split may run along subject/object (the sovereign Subject's self-assertion) rather than substrate/criterion, which would make the agency slot the real hinge this note under-theorizes.

The frame hardens into canon

Sean · updated 5 September 2026 · source on GitHub
Navigational frames - a feed, a map, a model's summary - begin as known approximations and harden, unnoticed, into the only reality anyone actually perceives, which is the deepest and most recursive circular error of the set because a frame mistaken for the world becomes unfalsifiable from the inside.

The idea

Every navigational frame - a social media feed, a map, an AI-generated summary - starts life as a known, useful approximation: a compression of something more complicated, adopted because it works well enough. The specific failure this row names is that such frames tend to harden, gradually and without anyone deciding it should happen, into the only reality anyone actually perceives - the map is mistaken for the territory, and people, in the model's own phrase, "drive into the lake." The practical acceptance is that navigating by frames is unavoidable; what can be built in is friction and reminders that keep the frame visible as a frame, and a deliberate cultivation of frame-literacy - the discipline of remembering that the map is not the territory, practiced rather than assumed. This is named as the deepest circular error of the whole set because it recurses: a frame mistaken for the world is unfalsifiable from the inside, since the approximation's own errors become, for whoever is inside the frame, simply how reality is.

Where it comes from

One row ("The frame hardens into canon") of a working table of bets for the World Machines project: a running list of structural errors in how today's fixes for the Divergence Machine's limitations themselves fail, each paired with a falsifiable, near-term bet about how the failure will surface.

How settled this is

A working hypothesis; the associated bet is that over-reliance harms in navigation, medicine, and hiring trigger explainability and contestability mandates, and that "automation complacency" becomes a recognized, named incident category.

Connections

  • substrate-and-criterion-split - "an age cannot see its own reoccupations as reoccupations" is the historical-epoch version of exactly this claim about frames - a structural blindness that recurs at every scale from a single feed to a whole civilization's self- understanding
  • grammar-of-compelling-names - embeddings offering resemblance-at-a- distance with the vestments of naturalist legitimacy is a specific case of a frame (cosine similarity as a number) hardening into an unquestioned kind of truth

Open

Whether frame-literacy can actually be taught and sustained as a discipline at scale, or whether the hardening this row describes is close to inevitable once a frame becomes convenient enough, is left unresolved.

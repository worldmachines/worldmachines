Illegibility as Elite Defense

Commons · Synthesis · updated 24 September 2026 · source on GitHub
Carey's material inverts the wiki's usual polarity: modernist difficulty and post-structuralist theory are deliberate illegibility cultivated by a losing incumbent elite against a democratizing expansion of legibility, which means strategic illegibility has no inherent political sign — it belongs to whoever is being outnumbered.

The Problem This Page Solves

Across this wiki, legibility and illegibility carry a stable political sign. Legibility is what power does to people — the cadastre, the surname, the statistical mean, the algorithmic average (legibility-as-machine-core, Legibility). Illegibility is the counter-move of the dominated: the thicket, the iltifat, the refusal to be mapped (The Thicket, capture-resistance). legibility-vs-complexity-the-master-tension states the pairing at its most general.

Carey's batch breaks that pairing, and no single note in it says so. Each note observes a local legibility fact; the connection between them is the finding.

The Notes and What Each Contributes

  • carey-intellectuals-and-masses-preface gives the mechanism: modernist difficulty as a purpose-built device to "exclude these newly educated (or 'semi-educated') readers."
  • carey-intellectuals-and-masses-ch1-revolt-of-the-masses gives it as a stated program rather than an inference — Eliot's poets who "must be difficult," New Verse founded against mass values, and Ortega's Dehumanization of Art openly welcoming modern art's function of sorting the public into "illustrious" and "vulgar" castes.
  • carey-intellectuals-and-masses-ch4-natural-aristocrats shows the same move in doctrine rather than form: esoteric knowledge (Lawrence, Yeats), initiate rapture (Bell), divine sanction (Huxley, Greene, Steiner) — grounds for distinction chosen because they are unverifiable from outside.
  • carey-intellectuals-and-masses-ch5-gissing-ineducable-masses supplies the mirror image, aimed downward: physiognomy and the bookshelf as a scheme for making human worth instantly readable and permanently classifiable, prior to any encounter with the person.
  • carey-intellectuals-and-masses-ch7-wells-against-wells supplies the other mirror image, and the era's largest one: after 1910 Wells abandons the freedom register for system, and "once system is accepted, categorization follows."
  • carey-intellectuals-and-masses-postscript supplies the proof of pattern rather than accident: when television undid modernism's occasion and poets after Eliot dropped the difficulty requirement, the barrier was rebuilt from new materials — post-structuralist "theory," its deliberate unintelligibility and its doctrine that biography and "human interest" are irrelevant, continuing straight from Bell and Ortega.

The Connection

Put those six together and a single structure appears that none of them states alone.

1. The threatened elite chose illegibility, and it worked. Universal education is a legibility project of the ordinary modernity-machine kind: a standardized, schooled, countable population. Its unintended output was a readership the literary intelligentsia had not authorized and could not gatekeep. The response was not to reassert control over the newly legible mass. It was to become unreadable — to relocate the boundary of culture from who is permitted to read to who can decode. Compulsory schooling cannot be repealed; difficulty needs no legislation.

2. The same generation deployed maximal legibility downward. Gissing's physiognomy-and-bookcase taxonomy and Wells's Paris index are the same people, in the same decades, insisting on total readability of others. Illegibility upward and legibility downward are not two positions; they are one asymmetry — the definition of a caste boundary maintained from the top.

3. Therefore the sign is not intrinsic. Strategic illegibility is not the property of the dominated. It is the property of whoever is losing a numbers contest while retaining the power to define the terrain. The literary intelligentsia was outnumbered but still owned the criteria of value; obscurity converted a demographic defeat into an aesthetic victory. The wiki's existing account — illegibility as the thicket, the refuge of the outlier — describes one use of a technique that has at least two.

4. Persistence across substrate change is the tell. The postscript's evidence matters most here. If modernist difficulty were an aesthetic development that incidentally excluded, it would not have been replaced by a functionally equivalent barrier once media democratization dissolved it. That the exclusion outlived its stated rationale, twice, is what identifies it as a structural response rather than a style.

Consequences for the Framework

  • legibility-vs-complexity-the-master-tension needs a sign-neutral formulation. That page's calibrated legibility is the right goal, but the calibration question is legible to whom, and at whose insistence. A demand for difficulty and a demand for transparency can both be exclusion moves depending on who is outnumbered.
  • A gramsci-gap reading. The interregnum here is a legitimacy gap in cultural authority: the old warrants (birth, ecclesiastical sanction, monopoly on literacy) expired and nothing organic replaced them. The natural-aristocracy doctrines of carey-intellectuals-and-masses-ch4-natural-aristocrats are morbid symptoms in the strict sense — improvised, self-contradictory replacements manufactured under pressure. Difficulty is what an incumbent does when it can no longer set the terms of entry.
  • A divergence-machine caution. Proliferating, non-convergent readership is the push-machine texture the DM is supposed to celebrate. Carey's period shows the incumbent culture experiencing exactly that proliferation as ontological threat and answering with a retreat into a curated, defended, deliberately small space. Divergence and elite retreat can produce identical surface phenomena — many incompatible cultural grammars — from opposite motives, and the-world-machines-canon's diagnostic filters should be able to tell them apart.

Ortega's Revolt: Distinction by Discipline, Not Difficulty

This page cites Ortega's Dehumanization of Art as an open program of sorting the public by who can decode modern art. The batch does not include that essay and cannot adjudicate Carey's reading of it, but The Revolt of the Masses (ortega-revolt-of-the-masses) marks the elite boundary differently, and the difference qualifies the pattern above.

  • The marker is self-demand, not decoding. The select man is “the man who demands more of himself than the rest” (ortega-revolt-of-the-masses-ch01-the-reality-of-the-masses); noble life is self-imposed obligation — “To live as one likes is plebeian; the noble man aspires to order and law” (ortega-revolt-of-the-masses-ch07-noble-life-and-vulgar-life); a barbarian is anyone “not concerned with adjusting himself to truth” (n. 23, ortega-revolt-of-the-masses-endnotes).
  • Culture is defined as appealable standards. Barbarism is the absence of “a series of standards to which it is possible to appeal” (ortega-revolt-of-the-masses-ch08-why-the-masses-intervene-in-everything) — a demand for shared, public criteria rather than for opacity.
  • Specialist narrowing is condemned, not prized. The “learned ignoramus,” competent only in a tiny corner and dismissive of wider curiosity as “dilettantism,” is Ortega's prototype of the mass-man (ortega-revolt-of-the-masses-ch12-the-barbarism-of-specialization) — the opposite of treating esoteric competence as a badge.
  • The style claim (Guerrero's). Guerrero presents Ortega's prose as transparent rather than “mystical and hermetic,” governed by the maxim “In philosophy, clarity is courtesy” (ortega-revolt-of-the-masses-essays-introduction) — translator's advocacy, but it points the same way.

Consequence. If Carey is right about Dehumanization, Ortega used both strategies in different decades or registers — illegibility in aesthetics, discipline-and-standards in politics and ethics. Either way, Revolt is an elite defense that works by raising the cost of self-exemption rather than the cost of access, which is a third option alongside the two asymmetries this page describes; whether it escapes the caste logic or just relocates it is left open (see the-mass-as-fiction, mass-man).

The Birth of Thickets (part 3 of 3)

Commons · Contributions · updated 19 July 2026 · source on GitHub
Contribution essay by aneesh, submitted to World Machines.

The Space and Place vocabulary, and the formula place is security, space is freedom, come from Yi-Fu Tuan’s Space and Place: The Perspective of Experience (1977). The wider project this essay sits inside is spatial epistemology, knowing as a spatial act, and the Place-series that builds the vocabulary runs through Thinking with Places, Problems are Places, Questions are Spaces, The Shelter as Epistemic Engine, and We Need Homes in the Delta Quadrant.

The structural move at the centre of this essay is Bayo Akomolafe’s, from The Untimely, a talk he gave to the Long Now Foundation on 5 May 2026. His argument runs on time rather than space: the untimely is not an alternative clock but the interval the clock cannot count, the excess the machinery secretes by running. To create an alternative, he says, is to repeat the logic of what you are escaping from; sanctuary is not something you can schedule and build, but what the world conspires to make with the cracks in us. I have run the same operation on the other axis. He escapes through time; I have tried to escape through space, taking his structure and declining his fatalism, since the niche opens without you but occupying it is work.

The world-machines framework (the Modernity Machine, the Divergence Machine, the Liveness era, and the Dawn, Day and Dusk phases a machine passes through) is Venkatesh Rao’s, and this essay assumes familiarity with his Contraptions writing on the topic, in particular The Divergence Machine. Two moves here are mine rather than his. Reading the platform as a Dusk-phase legibility apparatus processing Divergence output runs against his account, in which divergence brackets modernity and renders it irrelevant rather than being recaptured by it. And the framework’s table gives the Divergence Machine a Day lasting to 2400, which the closing wager declines to believe.

The interregnum, and monsters as the morbid symptoms that breed in it, are Rao’s, from The Gramsci Gap (Contraptions, 11 January 2025), which also supplies the move this essay turns on: that an interregnum in time can be replaced by an interregnum in space. His monsters there are strictly pathological and want exorcising. The generative sense is Akomolafe’s, from I, Coronavirus. Mother. Monster. Activist. (15 May 2020) and from interviews where he defines a crack as life in excess of itself, too much possibility abiding in a form for the form to hold it, and the monster as reality reworking itself. The trickster who straddles worlds and upsets every enterprise of closure is his too. Rao arrives somewhere adjacent a year after the Gap essay, in Engineering Liveness (Contraptions, 31 January 2026), where slightly monstrous people carry unseemly levels of liveness. Reading the two senses as one excess sorted two ways is mine, and neither of them makes that claim.

Pre-commodified is Sachin’s, from “LLMs Pre-Commodify Ideas” (Summer Lightning, 12 June 2026): when enough people work the same latent space, an idea surfaces everywhere at once and arrives already commodified, so that provenance rather than origination becomes the scarce thing.

The thicket is from my own The Architecture of Resistance, and the trilogy it belongs to runs from Logic of the Thicket and the Unsearchable Web through that essay to The Deep Dark Terroir of the Soul. The cost-collapse argument is from The Lightening of Intent, and Not My Lever promised this essay.

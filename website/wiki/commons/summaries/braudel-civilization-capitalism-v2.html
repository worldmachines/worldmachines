Civilization and Capitalism, 15th–18th Century, Vol. II: The Wheels of Commerce (Fernand Braudel)

Commons · Summaries · updated 24 September 2026 · source on GitHub
The second volume of Fernand Braudel's Civilization and Capitalism, which climbs from material life to the market economy and capitalism, arguing across markets, trade circuits, production, the trading hierarchy and society as a 'set of sets' that capitalism is not the market writ large but an opaque 'anti-market' zone of privilege, and closing on three conditions (a market economy, a dynastic hierarchical society, world trade) that together let capitalism develop.

The Wheels of Commerce (English edition 1982, translated by Siân Reynolds) is the second volume of fernand-braudel's Civilization and Capitalism trilogy. Volume I (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v1) weighed the ground floor of material life. This volume climbs "upstairs from the ground-level of material life" to the market economy and then to "the highest level of all, the action of capitalism" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-foreword). It is where the upper two floors of braudel-three-storey-house are actually built, and where Braudel's most contested claim is argued at length: the market economy and capitalism are not the same activity at different scales.

The central distinction

  • The market economy is transparent, competitive and rule-governed. Its model instance is the town or village market, "the most direct and transparent form of exchange, the most closely supervised and the least open to deception," selling "eyeball to eyeball" with almost no credit (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-elementary-markets-and-town-intervention). Braudel's empirical test for it is synchronized price movement: a market economy exists "when prices in the markets of a given area fluctuate in unison," a condition he finds across Europe by the twelfth century (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-locating-the-market).
  • Capitalism is "a shadowy zone, a twilight area of activities by the initiated," at root "an accumulation of power ... a form of social parasitism" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-foreword). It is "the zone of the anti-market, where the great predators roam and the law of the jungle operates," which Braudel names "the real home of capitalism," then as now (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-locating-the-market).
  • Below both lies a "non-economy" of self-sufficient life that capitalism's roots reach but never fully penetrate (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-locating-the-market). For the 80–90% of the population who were peasants, the market was at most an occasional presence, and reconstructing life "below the level of the market" is named as unfinished work (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-market-statistics-labour-market-beneath-market).

Braudel owns the anachronism of the word "capitalism" for 1400–1800. He needed one word for "this zone which is not the true market economy, but indeed often its exact opposite" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-foreword), and he refuses calls to ban it: "if capitalism is thrown out of the door, it comes in through the window" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-capital-capitalist-capitalism).

Method

The foreword names "observation — repeated until the eyes ached," borrowings from the human sciences, and above all "systematic comparison" over la longue durée. Because pre-industrial systems changed slowly, comparable cases across centuries become "virtually a series of experiments" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-foreword). In practice this means a vocabulary history of capital, capitalist and capitalism as a check on anachronism (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-capital-capitalist-capitalism), price series set against Polanyi's "almost theological taste for definition" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-locating-the-market), a call for "a methodical model, the model of a model" of profit rates on the lines of Simiand's and Labrousse's price history (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-industrial-profits-overland-transport), and a steady refusal of single causes: "economics played a part, politics played a part, society played a part, and culture and civilization played a part" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-capitalist-mentality-and-long-distance-trade).

The chapter arc

Foreword. The three-storey plan, the defence of the word, the comparative method, and the destination: a final chapter on society as a "set of sets," "outside which nothing can have full meaning." Braudel also admits rewriting the chapters four or five times (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-foreword).

1. The wheels of trade: markets, shops, fairs, Exchanges. The elementary market is ancient and nearly world-wide. Towns seized and policed it (a Sicilian vendor overcharging by "a single grana" could be sent to the galleys), and London's growth ("going to eat up all England") dissolved its face-to-face transparency into chains of intermediaries (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-elementary-markets-and-town-intervention). Counting markets (Everitt's roughly 800 English market towns) gives a hierarchy of specialization, not a law. Private trading grows up around the supervised market, and land, money and finally labour pass "through the narrow opening of the market." The labour market is "the most important, from the point of view of this book" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-market-statistics-labour-market-beneath-market). The shopkeeper becomes "a capitalist in a very small way" through credit, not scale, and Braudel borrows Marx's money-goods-money formula to mark the change. Pedlars persist as "a way of cocking a snook at established authority" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-shops-and-pedlars). Fairs are pyramids, with goods at the base and "the active money market" at the summit. At the Piacenza fairs roughly sixty Genoese bankers settled bills representing "the entire wealth of Europe" with "no merchandise" and "very little cash" present. Fairs then gave way to the Europe of warehouses (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-fairs-and-warehouses). The Exchange's cradle is the Mediterranean, not Amsterdam. Amsterdam added "blank" buying, and brokers' "kite-flying" before official quotation (1747) shows opacity used for extraction. China invented paper money centuries earlier, but it "did not play the role in China that it did in the West as the accelerator of capitalism" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-the-exchanges). Outside Europe, Braudel uses Skinner's market hexagons, the Armenian agent Hovhannes's credit-financed eleven-year circuit, and India's sarafs to contest Van Leur's picture of Asian "pedlars" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-world-outside-europe-markets-and-bankers). The chapter closes by periodizing four European centuries. The eighteenth century is the moment "the market was faced with the anti-market." Braudel names China "the most aberrant case" and warns that Europe invented historians and used them as evidence for itself, which biases any comparison (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-few-exchanges-many-fairs-concluding-hypotheses).

2. The market's mechanics and geography. Trade circuits "only work when the connection is unbroken." Bills of exchange closed them. Family firms, factors, subsidiaries and commission agents, held together by trust rather than law, let merchants act at a distance (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-merchants-trade-circuits-return-journeys). Merchant diasporas (Italian "nations," Armenians, Jews, Banyans) are explained as "controlling minorities." Braudel argues that "the social machinery itself" reserves suspect but necessary trades to outsiders, and that "a minority ... was a solid and ready-made network" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-merchant-networks-trading-empires). Trading profit rises with distance and is assured for "royal merchandise." Supply versus demand is "a question to which there is really no answer" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-trading-profits-supply-demand). Every capitalist chain has a "high point," rarely at production, and Europe "bombarded" the silver-hoarding "receptacle-countries" of India and China (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-markets-and-their-geography). The balance of trade, crude as it is, maps domination: permanent deficit delivered Bengal to "primitive accumulation" and China to opium, "paid in smoke" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-national-economies-balance-of-trade). The chapter ends on the three-tier architecture in its clearest form (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-locating-the-market).

3. "Production: or Capitalism away from Home." Capitalism was "a world apart ... in relation to a noncapitalism of immense proportions," most at home "in the sphere of circulation." Kuznets's ceiling of roughly 20% savings bounds pre-industrial capital formation (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-capital-capitalist-capitalism). Urban money flowed into land but seldom made capitalist agriculture, because "unless all these conditions were fulfilled ... it was not capitalist in the proper sense." The seigniorial regime survived by renegotiating what it took (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-land-money-seigniorial-regime). The second serfdom and the sugar plantations were not capitalist enterprises. They were machinery for merchants in Amsterdam, Gdańsk, Bordeaux and London, "the masters of the machine" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-second-serfdom-american-plantations). Capitalist agriculture on the Norfolk template was "a genuine but minority current" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-heart-of-europe-capitalist-agriculture). Braudel adopts Bourgin's fourfold typology of pre-industry, from family workshop to factory, and reads simple tools as a response to cheap labour (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-fourfold-classification-pre-industry). Merchant capital captured the guilds through the Verlagssystem and, in the mines, produced the first wage proletariat. At the same time, "the removal of investment is as characteristic of capital as investment" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-merchants-guilds-verlagssystem). Manufactories grew under mercantilist privilege, and Saint-Gobain's capital drifted to noble rentiers (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-mining-manufactories-finance). Industrial profits were "dwarfed by trading profits." Road carriage moved about five times the tonnage of waterways and was left unrationalized because it suited merchants (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-industrial-profits-overland-transport). The verdict on capitalism's inroads into production, transport included, is "pretty negative" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-river-sea-transport-balance-sheet).

4. "Capitalism on Home Ground." Specialization multiplies at the bottom of the trading hierarchy while the wholesaler refuses to specialize. Access to interest-bearing outside credit is "the crucial dividing line" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-trade-hierarchy-and-success). Western banking took off three times (Florence, Genoa, Amsterdam) and collapsed each time before the nineteenth century (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-credit-banking-and-money). Capitalist superiority was strategy, information and rule-bending, not "mentality." Braudel's evidence ranges from pepper's fivefold-to-fifteenfold markup to Dunkerque's rigged quay rule (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-capitalist-mentality-and-long-distance-trade). International monopoly, the "currency snare" of gold and silver against copper, and Hope & Co.'s failed cochineal corner support the claim that capitalism's defining trait is its "unlimited flexibility" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-monopolies-currency-and-profits). The firm's legal evolution from commenda through compagnia and commandite to joint stock outran practice, and most firms stayed patriarchal households (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-individual-firms-and-joint-stock). The great companies had Venetian and Iberian precedents and depended on a "rule of three": state, capital and trading zone (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-great-merchant-companies). The chapter closes by locating the frontier between market and capitalism in transparency against speculation. Baltic grain "changed its nature" in an Amsterdam warehouse, and a single commodity can occupy all three levels at once (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-threefold-division).

5. "Society or the 'set of sets'." Society is a "set of sets": economy, hierarchy, state and culture, none reducible to the others. Every society is hierarchical and plural, and power concentrates in a tiny elite by a "structural law that admits of no exception" that Braudel states without claiming to explain it (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-opening-hierarchies-vertical-elevation). Elite turnover is crisis-punctuated and loosely synchronized across Europe. Pirenne's rule that merchant families last two or three generations holds for families, but capital survives by moving from merchant to banker to financier to rentier (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-social-mobility-pirenne). France's gentry hardened into the noblesse de robe, and merchant cities' discretion contrasts with courts' compulsory ostentation (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-gentry-noblesse-de-robe-luxury). Revolts were endemic and usually contained. The Enlightenment attacked noble privilege while "the privileges conferred by capital were somehow brushed aside" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-revolutions-workers-revolts-order). Beneath the wage-earners a sub-proletariat escaped only into new dependencies, and "to say society seems invariably to mean saying hierarchy" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-below-subsistence-climbing-out-of-hell). The state's three tasks, the state as a class settlement, and Castile's juros and asientos show state and capitalism as parallel hierarchies both resorting to monopoly (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-all-pervasive-state-borrowing-castile). England's unplanned financial revolution produced a perpetual, transferable national debt, and the financiers form a state-tied sector of capitalism (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-english-financial-revolution-financiers). France had "no public finances at all" and ran on the Ferme générale. Mercantilism is a label Braudel keeps while doubting it names any coherent doctrine (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-ferme-generale-mercantilism). The state was "an unfinished entity" that governed through society and culture. The true "power apparatus" is "something more than the state," and capitalism's instruments were largely borrowed from Islam (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-state-culture-incomplete-entity-islamic-model). The usury ban was dismantled by scholastic loopholes. Weber's Protestant ethic meets Calvin's measured letter and Catholic Genoa's 1.2% interest (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-christendom-usury-puritanism). Braudel explains the North's rise by geography and costs, rejects Sombart's rationalism thesis while siding with him on Quattrocento Florence, and ends on a Chinese-junks counterfactual and the thought that capitalism "no longer appears to us to be the last word in historical evolution" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-retrospective-geography-rationalism-florence). Capitalism outside Europe turns on whether a society lets an upper class accumulate and hand down wealth across generations. Only Europe and, partly, Japan passed that test (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-capitalism-outside-europe-norman-jacobs-politics).

By Way of Conclusion. Three conditions made "the capitalist process considered as a whole" possible. The first is a vigorous market economy, which is necessary but not sufficient, as China shows. The second is a hierarchical, dynastic society that allows accumulation across generations, which Europe and Japan met "in their own separate ways." The third is "the special and as it were liberating action of world trade," the hinge into Volume III's world-economies (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-conclusion).

Throughlines

  • Capitalism lives in circulation and keeps its options open. It occupied "channels of trade," not the whole economy (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-capital-capitalist-capitalism). It withdrew from mining, agriculture and industry whenever profits fell (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-merchants-guilds-verlagssystem, braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-mining-manufactories-finance), refused to specialize (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-trade-hierarchy-and-success), and slipped between trade, industry, banking and land "at a moment's notice" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-monopolies-currency-and-profits). See capitalism-keeps-the-floor-competitive for what this implies about the floor beneath it.
  • Opacity and information. Braudel repeatedly locates the anti-market where exchange is withdrawn from common view: the Piacenza club (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-fairs-and-warehouses), unpublished share prices (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-the-exchanges), merchant correspondence ("once information becomes public it loses 90% of its value") (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-capitalist-mentality-and-long-distance-trade), and the warehoused grain cargo (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-threefold-division).
  • State and capital as parallel, entangled hierarchies. They share hierarchy and monopoly (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-all-pervasive-state-borrowing-castile). Successive creditor groups (Fuggers, Genoese, Amsterdam houses) held states financially hostage (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-english-financial-revolution-financiers). The state "undoubtedly encouraged capitalism," but the formula "can be reversed" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-state-culture-incomplete-entity-islamic-model).
  • World-scale comparison against "pioneering genius." Markets, fairs, credit and merchant dynasties appear in Islam, India, China and Japan (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-world-outside-europe-markets-and-bankers, braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-merchant-networks-trading-empires). Bookkeeping, the bill of exchange and the commenda were "cultural goods, which travelled" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-state-culture-incomplete-entity-islamic-model). The China case recurs throughout. See braudel-china-precocity-puzzle.
  • No single cause. Braudel argues against Polanyi (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-locating-the-market), Weber and Sombart (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-christendom-usury-puritanism, braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-retrospective-geography-rationalism-florence) and Schumpeterian entrepreneurial spirit (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-trade-hierarchy-and-success). In each case he prefers several causes or a structural explanation.

Where it meets the World Machines frame

The notes flag the rhymes below as partial. The hedges are part of each claim.

  • legibility-as-machine-core, inverted again. The market floor's transparency is "a horizontal legibility among traders" that capitalism escapes rather than imposes. The predators gain their edge "by manufacturing opacity above an otherwise legible floor" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch04-threefold-division). The missing public quotation is what "lets insiders capture value from outsiders" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-the-exchanges). China's state patrols and registers wealth in order to intercept it, which is legibility used to contain accumulation rather than to expand it (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-capitalism-outside-europe-norman-jacobs-politics). See legibility-as-precondition-not-cause.
  • world-machines. The world-economies Volume III will study "loosely rhyme" with world machines, but they are defined by trade dominance and assigned no Dawn/Day/Dusk cycle (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-conclusion). The "power apparatus" as a self-reconstituting composite of hierarchies rhymes with a world machine's interlocking institutions, but it is "a synchronic sociological claim" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-state-culture-incomplete-entity-islamic-model). The set of sets is plural with shifting dominance, "a resonance ... of shape ... rather than mechanism" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-opening-hierarchies-vertical-elevation).
  • history-machine. Several things assemble from scattered practice long before anyone names them: the Verlagssystem (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-merchants-guilds-verlagssystem), England's financial revolution (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-english-financial-revolution-financiers), mercantilism (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-ferme-generale-mercantilism), and capitalism's borrowed toolkit (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-state-culture-incomplete-entity-islamic-model). Each note marks this as a rhyme in shape, not in scale or register.
  • decay-sequence. Seigniorial dues decayed into "a sixteenth of a capon" while the institution kept its power (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-land-money-seigniorial-regime). Guilds kept their "outward appearances" after the Verleger took over (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-merchants-guilds-verlagssystem). The usury ban became ornamental law (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-christendom-usury-puritanism). Medina del Campo was left "a husk" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-fairs-and-warehouses). Each fit is flagged as partial in scale or kind.
  • capture-resistance and Conatus, with limits. The floating population "deliberately avoids anything that might re-create fresh bonds," but it was "not a class but a rabble" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-below-subsistence-climbing-out-of-hell). The Gévaudan's cash-payment custom repelled the Verlagssystem (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-merchants-guilds-verlagssystem). Suppressed workers' organization "goes dark" in the record rather than disappearing (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-revolutions-workers-revolts-order). Controlling minorities turn exclusion into striving, but as "economic specialists ... under host-state tolerance," not capture-resistant communities (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-merchant-networks-trading-empires).
  • modernity-machine. The balance of trade is an accounting technology invented by territorial states, "squarely material for the modernity-machine's construction period" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch02-national-economies-balance-of-trade). The fifteenth-century state is "a parallel construction project" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-all-pervasive-state-borrowing-castile). The state still could not read its own budgets well into the eighteenth century (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch05-english-financial-revolution-financiers).
  • gramsci-gap and divergence-machine, loosely. Clearing centres migrated from Champagne to Amsterdam through disruptive gaps (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-fairs-and-warehouses). One European pressure produced "radically non-convergent" colonial forms (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch03-second-serfdom-american-plantations). The eighteenth-century split between consolidation at the summit and pedlars multiplying at the base "half-rhymes" with the modernity and divergence machines in friction, but it is "a synchronic dialectic within one economy" (braudel-civilization-capitalism-v2-ch01-few-exchanges-many-fairs-concluding-hypotheses).

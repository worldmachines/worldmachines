On Not Knowing and Paying Attention

Tim Ingold · 2023 · 9 reading notes · source id ingold-2023-possible-world

  • Abstract: Attention, Not-Knowing, and the Wisdom of a Possible World

    Wisdom is an attentional, anticipatory orientation to a necessarily uncertain, possibility-open world, while knowledge is intentional and predictive and mistakes not-knowing for mere ignorance.

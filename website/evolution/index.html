What evolutionary theory learned after 1995

Evolution is change in inherited variation across generations. Darwin’s Dangerous Idea made natural selection vivid as an algorithm. Genomes, ancient DNA, development, and long-running experiments have since shown a richer picture without discarding that core idea.

Start with the question you have · high-school biology is enough · no login

In one sentence: selection explains adaptive fit, while mutation, inheritance, development, chance, migration, ecology, and history all help determine what actually evolves.

The causal picture

1 · VariationMutation creates changes; recombination reshuffles them; migration brings them in.
→
2 · DevelopmentGenes, cells, bodies, and surroundings make traits.
→
3 · ConsequencesSome inherited traits lead to more descendants in that setting.
→
4 · Population changeSelection and chance alter which variants become common.

The environment is not merely a backdrop: organisms and cultures modify it, and history limits which paths remain available.

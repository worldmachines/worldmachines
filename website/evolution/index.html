What evolutionary theory learned after 1995

Darwin’s Dangerous Idea made natural selection vivid as an algorithm. Thirty years of genomes, ancient DNA, evo-devo, long experiments, and sharper models have not retired that idea. They have shown where chance, development, ecology, cooperation, and multiple inheritance channels enter the causal picture.

Six lessons · about 75 minutes · high-school biology is enough · no login

The course’s one-sentence thesis: selection explains adaptive fit, but evolution is the joint outcome of variation, inheritance, development, selection, drift, gene flow, and history—at more than one biological scale.
